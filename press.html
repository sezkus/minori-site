Minori — press kit

Everything here is free to use for editorial coverage. No sign-in, no request form.

Minori is a digital junk journal for a baby's first years — a keepsake book you make by hand, not a template photo album. Tap the cover and it opens; every page is a blank sheet you fill with photos cut into shapes, handwritten notes, washi tape, stickers and finger drawing.

What makes it different

  • The book is native. No web view anywhere — the cover lifts, the spine turns and the leaves fall in a hand-written engine, and photo filters run on the GPU through Skia and Metal.
  • The cut-out is on device. One tap lifts the subject out of its background with Vision, on the phone; nothing is uploaded to do it.
  • Nothing leaves the phone. No account, no content server, no ads, no cross-app tracking. Books back up as a single file to Files or iCloud Drive and export as a print-ready PDF.
  • 689 stickers in 30 sets, eleven of them drawn for Minori, plus patterns, tapes, frames and photo shapes generated in code.

New in 1.2

  • Home Screen and Lock Screen widget — the baby's name and age, the pages and photos the book holds, with the cover's own colour. The age is formatted by the system, so it reads correctly in every one of the 24 languages.
  • Siri Shortcuts — "Add a memory" and "Open my book", localized into all 24 languages rather than English only.
  • Camera — take the photo without leaving the page.
  • Sixteen new languages, taking Minori from 8 to 24.
